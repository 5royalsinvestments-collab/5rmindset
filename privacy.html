Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

5R Mindset (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a brand of 5Royals Investments LLC. We publish youth-sports mental-game journals and resources. This policy explains what information we collect on 5rmindset.com, how we use it, and your choices. By using our site or joining our email list, you agree to this policy.

Who this site is for

Our journals are designed for young athletes, but this website and our email list are intended for parents, coaches, and other adults (18+) who buy and use our products. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us — such as your first name and email address when you request our free download or join our newsletter, or the message and contact details you send us (for example, a bulk-order inquiry).
  • Basic technical data — standard information your browser sends (such as general location and device type) that our hosting provider records to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To send you the free resource you requested.
  • To send you emails about our journals, tips, and offers — which you can stop anytime.
  • To respond to your inquiries (including bulk/team orders).
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.

How your information is shared

We use trusted service providers to run our business, and we only share what's needed for them to do their job:

  • Email — we use MailerLite to store our list and send emails.
  • Hosting — our site is hosted on GitHub Pages / Cloudflare.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for others' advertising.

Cookies

Our site is a simple static website and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting and security providers may set basic cookies needed to deliver the site safely.

Your choices & rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Access or delete your information — email us and we'll take care of it.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as you're subscribed or as needed to provide our products and comply with our obligations. When you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, we remove it from our active list.

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with an updated date.
